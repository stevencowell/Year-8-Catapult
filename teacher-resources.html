Assessment authority

The official handbook confirms Project and Practical Task 60%, Folio 25% and Design Knowledge 15%, due at the end of Semester 1 and Semester 2. It also says in-class activities contribute to the overall grade; the exact assessed activities and method remain Teacher to confirm. Catapult-specific formal notifications are absent from the supplied sources and remain a controlled draft/rework item. Do not treat the student evidence guide as formal notice.
