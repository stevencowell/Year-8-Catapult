How the course works

Learn, apply, make and explain

Learn the idea

Each module teaches three named ideas with concept diagrams, examples, vocabulary and checks.

Apply it purposefully

Complete a short concept-linked activity before the longer module evidence response.

Make under teacher direction

The website prepares you for practical work. Your teacher controls demonstrations, tools, safety and test conditions.
