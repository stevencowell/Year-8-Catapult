Stage 4 Mandatory Technology

Assessment evidence guide

Use this page to understand the authorised school schedule and organise your Catapult evidence. Your teacher's issued notification controls the exact conditions, marks, date and submission details.

This is not a formal task notification

The 2026 school handbook confirms the three task categories, semester timing and weightings below. It also says in-class activities contribute to the overall grade; this website provides practice and evidence fields, but only your teacher can confirm which activities are assessed and how. Catapult-specific notifications were not in the supplied sources. Follow the notification issued by your teacher if it differs from this preparation guide.
